Minister of Youth Development and Empowerment Calls on Christian Council


The Minister of Youth Development and Empowerment, Hon. George Opare Addo has called on the General Secretary of the Christian Council of Ghana, Rev. Dr. Cyril Fayose in Accra.


His visit was aimed at seeking the collaboration of the Council in dealing with issues concerning the youth of Ghana, particularly drug abuse and addiction.


Oh his part, Rev. Dr. Fayose expressed the willingness of the Council to help government in addressing the problems confronting te youth of the Country.
